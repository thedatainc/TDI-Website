Our story

Why TDI exists.

For twenty years I designed and delivered systems for telecom operators, energy utilities and public safety agencies. The hardware and software were rarely the problem. The problem was the data behind them: scattered across spreadsheets, PDFs and legacy systems, and trusted by nobody.

I kept seeing the same pattern. Large consultancies wrote the strategy and left. Software vendors sold a product and expected the client to fit around it. Nobody stayed to do the hard, unglamorous work of making the data right and building the system people actually use every day.

We started The Data Inc to be that team. Every project has a co-founder accountable for it, from the first workshop to go-live and beyond. Our engineers and analysts build what the work needs, and when we see the same problem in more than one place, we turn it into a product.

— Atif H Syed, Co-Founder
