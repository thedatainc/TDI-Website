The challenge

A furniture catalogue can contain dimensions, finishes, materials, variants, imagery and channel-specific attributes. The same product must meet different marketplace requirements. Incomplete fields and inconsistent values create exceptions that need repeated investigation and correction.

How OMNIcat works

OMNIcat brings catalogue preparation, validation and marketplace maintenance into one repeatable workflow. Machine learning matches supplier attributes to each marketplace's fields and classifies products into the right categories, with people reviewing low-confidence results. Exceptions are tracked so corrections are fixed at the source and carried into future updates.

In practice

A dining table has its dimensions recorded in different formats across supplier files. OMNIcat standardises the values, flags missing measurements and prepares the fields each marketplace needs. A classification model places the table in the right category on each channel, and any match it is unsure of goes to a person to confirm.

Capabilities
  • Catalogue structuring, attribute mapping and data-quality checks
  • Machine-learning attribute matching and product classification, with human review
  • Listing preparation and maintenance across Amazon, Walmart, Wayfair and more
  • Exception handling and inventory-data coordination
How a pilot is measured

OMNIcat supports catalogue, listing and quality-control operations across channels including Amazon, Walmart and Wayfair. No reduction in rejection rates, sales uplift or publishing-time improvement is claimed.
