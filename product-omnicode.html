The challenge

A discharge summary can contain the reason for admission, several diagnoses, procedures, historical conditions and uncertain findings in one narrative. Coders must locate the relevant evidence, resolve ambiguities and record a defensible decision. Missing documentation can lead to repeated queries and delayed completion.

How OMNIcode works

A review workspace brings approved source documents into one view, highlights potentially relevant diagnoses and procedures, and links every suggestion to the supporting passage. A coder can accept, amend or reject a suggestion, or raise a documentation query. The system preserves the source, reviewer and decision history.

In practice

A record mentions a possible diagnosis during admission but does not clearly establish it at discharge. OMNIcode flags the ambiguity and prepares a review query instead of silently treating the condition as confirmed.

Capabilities
  • Document ingestion and extraction from approved sources
  • Evidence-linked suggestions with uncertainty made visible
  • Coder review queue, documentation queries and decision history
  • Role-based access and an evaluation workspace using synthetic or appropriately authorised records
How a pilot is measured

The intended benefit is less time searching through records and a clearer trail behind coding decisions. A pilot would measure review time, agreement with expert-reviewed records, unsupported suggestions and query resolution. No coding accuracy, financial return or time saving is claimed.
